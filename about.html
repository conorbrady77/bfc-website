How we work

How a project runs, in eight stages

Every project follows the same eight stages, with written confirmation from you at the stages that matter.

01

Process Assessment

We agree which process is worth fixing and what success looks like, and confirm it with you in a short email.

02

Process Definition

We write down, step by step, how the process works today, including the exceptions. The person who runs it confirms it is accurate.

03

Solution Design and Proposal

We write down exactly how the system will work: its rules, what it covers and what it does not. Nothing is built until you confirm it in writing.

You sign off
04

Development

We build the system, tested on sample data first.

05

Testing (UAT)

We run the system against real past cases to check the results are accurate. Nothing goes live until you sign off.

You sign off
06

Deployment

The system goes live with a small batch, not everything at once, and we watch it closely for the first few weeks.

07

Evaluation

You receive a written statement of whether the target from stage 1 was met, partly met or not met.

08

Ongoing Monitoring

Automated monitoring alerts us to any error. On an agreed schedule we review the results with you and recommend next steps.

Nothing is built before stage 3 is confirmed, and nothing goes live before stage 5 is signed off.

How we charge

A fixed fee, never a metered bill

Step 01 is free. You see a small project built and running before you commit to anything.

Step 02 is two months at a reduced fee. We deliver every month, so you see what a month of working together looks like.

Step 03 is a fixed monthly fee, agreed with you after step 2. Usage costs such as AI and hosting are included in that fee, so you never receive a bill that changes with usage.
