Case study

UK Trade Finance Company

We are building the start of every deal for a UK trade finance lender, so the credit team begins each one from a prepared file.

The start of a deal

What we are building

Every deal starts with a broker's application and a set of supporting documents. We are building the process that handles that start: the documents land in one organised folder, the credit team is alerted straight away, and the first credit memo is drafted for review.

Different systems talk to each other, the information is pulled in and analysed, and problems are flagged early.

What the system does

From application to first memo

DOCUMENTS

Documents in one folder

The broker's application and every supporting document land in one organised folder.

CONNECTIONS

Systems connected

We connect open banking and credit reporting systems through their APIs, so information is pulled in directly.

ANALYSIS

Information analysed

The information is analysed and organised, so the team starts from a prepared file, not a pile of documents.

RED FLAGS

Red flags raised early

Problems are flagged at the start of the process, so no time is wasted on leads that are going nowhere.

ALERTS

Credit team alerted

The credit team is alerted straight away, with the file ready.

CREDIT MEMO

First memo drafted

Every fact in the first credit memo carries a citation that can be checked. The credit team reviews it and makes the call.

Who decides

Your people make the call

The system gathers, drafts and flags. The credit team reviews the draft and makes the call.
